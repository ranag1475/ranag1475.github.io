🎛️ What Is mainstage-desktop?

If you build patches, set lists, or live performance rigs in Mainstage, your work lives in project folders. Those folders hold the preset and sample files that make each patch sound the way it does. When you rework a patch, the old preset and sample files get replaced, and the earlier version is gone.

mainstage-desktop solves that quietly. It is a small desktop helper that keeps your Mainstage project folders on disk and writes a dated copy of the preset and sample files into them before a patch change happens. Each copy carries the date it was made, so you can look back and see exactly what your folders looked like on any given day.

There is no complicated setup, no account to create, and no cloud service to sign into. It runs as a normal Windows application: download it, run it, point it at the folders you want watched, and carry on making music.

✨ What It Does for You

Every feature is built around one goal: your Mainstage project folders should never lose a working version of a preset or sample file.

  • Dated copies, automatically. Before a patch touches your files, mainstage-desktop writes a copy alongside them with the date attached. No manual saving, no remembering to do it.
  • Presets and samples together. Both kinds of files are covered, so a patch that swaps out a sample pack or rewrites a preset still has a working version on disk.
  • Stays inside your project folder. Copies are kept next to your project, not in a separate vault you have to hunt for later. Everything travels with the folder.
  • Plain files on disk. Nothing is compressed into a special format or locked in a database. The copies are ordinary files you can open, copy, or move like any other.
  • Light on your machine. The utility idles quietly in the background and only does work when something in your watched folders changes.
  • Straightforward window. One screen, a short list of folders, and a clear on or off state. There is nothing to configure if you do not want to.

📁 A Typical Session

You open a project you have been shaping for weeks. You decide the lead preset needs a cleaner top end. You tweak it, save it, and move on to the next patch. Later that evening the new version does not sit right in the mix.

With mainstage-desktop running, a dated copy of the preset as it existed before your edit is already sitting in the project folder. You open the folder, find the copy from earlier that day, and put it back. The change is undone in seconds, and the rest of the project is untouched.

The same applies to sample files. If a patch pulls in a different sample set, the earlier samples remain on disk under their own dated name, ready whenever you need them again.

❓ Common Questions

Do I need to know anything about Mainstage internals?

No. You point mainstage-desktop at a folder. It handles the rest.

Where do the dated copies go?

Inside the same project folder, next to the preset and sample files they came from.

Can I use it on more than one project folder?

Yes. Add as many folders as you like from the main window.

Does it change the sound of my patches?

No. It only writes copies of files. It does not edit, rewrite, or process anything you have made.
